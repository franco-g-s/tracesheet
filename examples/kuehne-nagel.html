Conventions

Revenue
The filed top line. Margins divide by it. Gross margin is gross profit ÷ revenue.
Capex
Purchases of property, plant and equipment plus purchases of intangible assets, both from the cash-flow statement: the common analyst definition. Both are held as positive outflows; a purchase line filed as negative gives n/m rather than being flipped. Intangible purchases count as zero only where the report prints no such line, or prints one line for both, which is then taken whole; otherwise capex is n/m. Free cash flow = operating cash flow − capex, used consistently in FCF growth, conversion, per share and yield.
FCF conversion
Free cash flow ÷ net income, both consolidated.
Earnings and equity
Attributable to the parent's shareholders for EPS, return on equity, payout ratio and book value per share. Consolidated (including minorities) for margins, return on assets and FCF conversion.
NOPAT
EBIT × (1 − effective tax rate), with effective rate = income tax ÷ profit before tax. Undefined when profit before tax ≤ 0.
Invested capital
Total equity + financial debt + lease liabilities − cash and equivalents. Financial debt is the filed total, or else current plus non-current borrowings. Both halves must be filed: a half the company has none of counts as zero only where the record declares it not printed; a half simply missing gives n/m.
Lease liabilities
The filed total, or else current plus non-current lease liabilities. A lease line counts as zero only where the record declares it not printed, so the zero is filed with its page; a lease line simply missing gives n/m, whatever the year. Before IFRS 16 (periods beginning before 1 January 2019) the balance sheet carried finance leases only, and those are what such years show. The first IFRS 16 year averages an opening balance with finance leases only against a closing balance with all leases.
Averages
Return on equity, on assets and on invested capital use the average of opening and closing balances. The first year shown, and any year after a gap, has no opening balance, so they are undefined there.
Net debt
Financial debt + lease liabilities − cash and equivalents. It may be negative (net cash).
Shares
Per-share flow figures (diluted EPS and FCF per share) use the diluted weighted average for the year. Book value per share is the exception: like market capitalisation, it uses year-end shares outstanding, net of treasury shares, a balance-sheet point in time, which keeps price-to-book consistent with market capitalisation. Every per-share figure and count is on one share basis across the years.
Dividends
Dividend per share is the ordinary dividend for the fiscal year, as proposed. Special dividends are shown separately, as special dividend per share. Payout ratio = ordinary dividend per share ÷ diluted EPS. Buybacks are the cash-flow line.
EV
Market capitalisation + financial debt + lease liabilities + minorities − cash and equivalents, from the latest annual balance sheet.
Currency
Figures stay in the reporting currency. No exchange rate is fetched: when the share price is in another currency, the multiples are n/m (no exchange rate). A caller may supply a rate from the price's currency into the reporting currency; the price is then converted at it, and the rate is shown and sits in every multiple's trail.
Growth
Year on year: (this year − prior year) ÷ prior year. Compound annual growth over 5 and 10 years ends in the latest year. Both are undefined when the starting value is zero or negative; compound growth also when the ending value is.
Undefined
A zero or negative denominator, or a missing input, gives n/m with the reason. Nothing is clamped or imputed.
